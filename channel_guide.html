📌 How to read this channel Paper-trading notes for learning. Not investment advice and not a SEBI-registered service. Nobody here places orders for you. Check your own chart and risk before any trade; an option buyer can lose the whole premium. What gets posted 🟢 / 🔴 News alert — company news on an F&O stock read by AI: 🟢 good (CE side), 🔴 bad (PE side), strong or weak. 📈 / 📉 Live breakout — a watchlist stock crossed its chart line during market hours. 📣 Results reaction — a company filed results during market hours and its price moved at least half a normal day within 15 minutes. Paper test of a new rule: buy that side, no stop, sell 15:15 on day 3. 📈 Chart breakouts ~19:15 — evening scan of all NSE stocks + next day's 🎯 watchlist. 📋 Digest 08:50 · 15:40 · 21:00 — the day's news in one list. 📊 Scorecard 15:45 — how every suggestion did as a 1-lot paper trade, losses included. Reading a news alert • Top line: stock · news type · strength · time of the post. (tip) = a call seen in a tip channel, tracked to check it. • Price line: price, the first 15-min range, and a normal day's move (±%). • 📐 Chart n/5 — does the chart back this side? Trend vs 20/50 EMA, 200 EMA, RSI not stretched, at least 2% room to the next resistance/support, not already moved more than a normal day. 4–5 agrees · 3 mixed · 0–2 disagrees. • ⛔ F&O ban — no new positions allowed that day. Skip. • ⚠️ Results soon — option prices usually drop right after results (IV crush). • 📊 Last quarter's sales and profit vs the same quarter a year ago. • On holidays and weekends the alert shows the last close and the next trading day's plan. The trade rule (the only part that tested well) The news is not the entry. It only says which stock and which side to watch. • After 9:30, buy CE only if the first 15-min candle closed green and price breaks its high. PE: red candle, breaks its low. • Stop: the other side of that 15-min range. • Exit by 15:15. No overnight holds. • "Too late to chase" = already more than 1% past the trigger. Wait for a pull-back to it, or skip. • "Rule says skip" = the first candle went the other way. • Option line: the deepest in-the-money strike whose lot costs up to ₹20,000, with its live ask, delta and spread. ⚠️ wide spread = expensive to get in and out. Breakouts Score out of 7: volume, close near the day's high/low, 20/50 EMA, 200 EMA, RSI, room to the next level, not too far past the line. 📦 = a tight range broken on 2× normal volume. Plan: buy on the break or a dip back to the line, stop if it closes back past the line, hold up to 3 days. Honest status Every alert is paper-traded with real NSE bid/ask prices. Early results are roughly break-even and nothing is proven yet. Strong news has done better than weak news; tips have done worst. The 15:45 scorecard shows the real numbers every day.
